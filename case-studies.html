Mechanical workshop

Workshop hoist wired, tested and earning

The situation. A busy mechanical workshop needed a new two-post hoist powered up — safely, to standard, and without shutting the floor down for days.

What we did. Ran the new circuit, installed isolation and safety controls, wired and commissioned the hoist and tested everything under load.

The result. The hoist went straight to work. Cars up, cars down, no drama — and the crew never lost a booking.

New home build

Builder's power for a new home build

The situation. A new home build on the coast needed compliant builder's power before a single trade could start on site.

What we did. Installed the new meter board, arranged connection, and set up temporary builder's power with weather-rated protection.

The result. The site went live on schedule. Every trade that followed plugged in and got on with it — zero electrical hold-ups.
