Privacy Policy

The short version: we use your details to call you back about your job, and nothing else.

Last updated: August 2026

MVS Connect Electrical Services ("we", "us") respects your privacy and handles personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

What we collect

  • Contact details you give us through our enquiry forms, phone or email — such as your name, phone number, email address and suburb.
  • Job details you describe to us so we can quote and carry out work.
  • Website analytics — anonymous usage data collected via Google Analytics (pages visited, device type, general location). This does not identify you personally.

How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and provide quotes and electrical services.
  • To keep records required for licensing, warranty and safety compliance.
  • To improve our website and advertising (aggregate analytics only).

What we never do

  • We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information.
  • We do not add you to marketing lists you didn't ask for.

Third-party services

Our website uses Google Analytics and Google Maps, and our enquiry forms are processed through our customer-management platform. These providers store data in accordance with their own privacy policies. Form submissions are transmitted securely.

Cookies

This site uses basic cookies for analytics and to remember small preferences (like whether you've already seen our enquiry popup). You can disable cookies in your browser without losing access to the site.

Access & corrections

You can ask what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by emailing mvsconnect@outlook.com or calling 0434 194 554.
